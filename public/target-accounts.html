🔬 Methodology

Lead scoring — 6-factor model (Industry Fit 0-30, Engagement 0-25, Multi-Threading 0-15, Active Deals 0-20, Recency 0-10, Geo 0-10) with partner/reseller −10 penalty. Top 100 of 348 qualified USA/UK B2B companies in HubSpot, excluding Tier 1 customers.

Platform detection — fetched live HTML + headers from each domain (89 of 100 successful; 11 fetch-blocked). Parsed signatures: cdn.shopify.com, /static/frontend/, cdn11.bigcommerce.com, wp-content/plugins/woocommerce, Magento version paths, meta generators. Adobe Commerce marked when Magento Cloud / Enterprise markers detected.

Size & revenue estimates — known brands use public data; unknowns estimated from platform tier (Adobe Commerce implies Mid-Market, Shopify implies SMB). Directional only; verify before pitching.

Limitations — 11 domains refused our bot (marked "Could not fetch"). Platform detection is best-effort from public HTML; SaaS accounts with aggressive WAFs may be misclassified. Shopify Plus vs standard Shopify is hard to distinguish from homepage alone — ~2 confirmed Plus, others may be Plus too.
