Executive Summary

New-business deal creation is down 67.92% year-over-year, with 17 deals created YTD (Jan–Apr 2026) versus 53 across the full prior year. Pipeline quality is a concern: 10 of 12 closed deals were lost (16.7% win rate), anchored by two Mirakl integration wins for American Optical and Eyebobs in January. 5 open opportunities remain in flight — 3 in Discovery and 2 in MQL — with the two most recent (Titan Industries and Honeybee Hippie) both created on April 21. Focus the next two weeks on advancing the 5 open leads through Discovery and on top-of-funnel activity to close the YoY gap.
